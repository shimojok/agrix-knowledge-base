# Citrus Case Study  
### MBT55 × PBPE: Biological Suppression of HLB (Citrus Greening) and Regenerative Citrus Production

---

## 1. Background: HLB as the Most Devastating Disease in Citrus History

Huanglongbing (HLB), also known as Citrus Greening, is the single most destructive  
citrus disease globally. It has caused:

- **30–70% yield losses** in major citrus regions  
- Collapse of entire orchards  
- Billions of dollars in economic damage  
- No effective chemical cure  

HLB is caused by *Candidatus Liberibacter asiaticus* (CLas), transmitted by the  
Asian citrus psyllid. Once infected, trees suffer:

- Phloem blockage  
- Root dieback  
- Fruit deformation  
- Premature drop  
- Eventual tree death  

Conventional agrochemicals cannot reverse the systemic infection nor restore  
root function.

---

## 2. MBT55: A Biological Architecture That Reverses HLB-Driven Decline

MBT55/HMT238 provides a **5-layer ecological architecture** that restores  
root function, suppresses pathogen activity, and rebuilds tree immunity.

### **Layer 1: Root Microbiome Reconstruction**
HLB destroys >30% of the root system before visible symptoms appear.  
MBT55 rapidly rebuilds the rhizosphere by:

- Replacing pathogenic microbial communities  
- Increasing beneficial Bacillus, Lactobacillus, and Actinomycetes  
- Enhancing nutrient uptake and root respiration  

**Result: Root biomass recovery and restored phloem flow.**

---

### **Layer 2: Antagonistic Metabolites Against CLas-Associated Microbes**
MBT55 microbes produce:

- Organic acids  
- Antimicrobial peptides  
- Volatile compounds  

These metabolites suppress secondary pathogens that accelerate HLB decline  
(e.g., *Phytophthora*, *Diaporthe*, *Colletotrichum*).

**Result: Reduced pathogen load and stabilized canopy health.**

---

### **Layer 3: Micro-environment Control**
MBT55 optimizes:

- Soil pH  
- Rhizosphere oxygenation  
- Moisture balance  

This eliminates the anaerobic micro-niches where HLB-associated pathogens thrive.

**Result: A root-zone environment hostile to disease progression.**

---

### **Layer 4: Immune Priming (ISR/ISE)**
MBT55 activates systemic resistance pathways:

- Phenylpropanoid pathway  
- PR-protein expression  
- Cell wall lignification  

This strengthens the tree’s ability to compartmentalize infection.

**Result: Slowed systemic spread of CLas.**

---

### **Layer 5: Stress Tolerance Enhancement**
HLB-infected trees suffer from:

- Oxidative stress  
- Carbohydrate starvation  
- Hormonal imbalance  

MBT55 restores metabolic balance by:

- Increasing antioxidant enzyme activity  
- Improving carbohydrate partitioning  
- Enhancing drought and heat tolerance  

**Result: Trees regain vigor and fruiting capacity.**

---

## 3. Biological Outcomes Observed in Field Deployments

Across citrus regions (Asia, Latin America, Mediterranean), MBT55 application  
has demonstrated:

### **1) Root Biomass Recovery**
- +25–40% root mass  
- Improved feeder root density  
- Enhanced nutrient uptake

### **2) Canopy Regeneration**
- New flush growth  
- Reduced leaf mottling  
- Improved chlorophyll content

### **3) Fruit Quality Improvement**
- Higher Brix  
- More uniform fruit size  
- Reduced premature fruit drop

### **4) Yield Stabilization**
- 20–35% yield recovery in declining orchards  
- Extended productive lifespan of trees

---

## 4. Carbon and Environmental Impact

MBT55-driven regeneration produces measurable climate benefits:

### **1) Increased Biomass Carbon**
- Recovered canopy and root mass  
- Higher perennial carbon storage

### **2) Soil Organic Carbon (SOC) Increase**
- Enhanced microbial activity  
- Improved soil aggregation  
- Faster carbon turnover into stable SOC

### **3) Reduced Chemical Inputs**
- Lower pesticide and fertilizer use  
- Reduced N₂O emissions

These gains can be quantified as **Verified Carbon Units (VCUs)** through  
AGRIX + Azure MRV.

**Citrus purchasing becomes climate finance.**
