# 6. Recommendation Ranking Engine

ここ重要。

---

# 推薦順位

以下を統合評価：

|項目|意味|
|---|---|
|pathway_compensation|補完力|
|conversion_efficiency|変換効率|
|disease_relevance|疾病関連|
|microbiome_compatibility|腸内適合|
|anti_inflammatory_score|抗炎症|

---

# スコア式

RecommendationScore=PathwayCompensation\times ConversionEfficiency\times DiseaseRelevance\times MicrobiomeCompatibility
