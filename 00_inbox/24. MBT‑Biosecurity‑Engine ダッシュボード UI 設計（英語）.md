### 1.3 Crops & Disease

**Main indicators（カード or グラフ）:**

- **Disease Loss Reduction Index** [ \frac{L_{\text{before}} - L_{\text{after}}}{L_{\text{before}}} ]
    
- **Yield Gain (%)** [ \frac{Y_{\text{after}} - Y_{\text{before}}}{Y_{\text{before}}} ]
    
- **Quality Premium Score** [ Q = w_1 B + w_2 T + w_3 P - w_4 D ]
    

**UI:**

- Crop selector（Coffee / Citrus / Cacao / Grapes / etc.）
- Before/After bar charts
- Time‑series line charts（per season）

---

### 1.4 Livestock & One Health

**Indicators:**

- Infection Rate Reduction (%)
- Culling Avoided (head)
- Antibiotic Use Reduction (%)
- Livestock Biosecurity Score [ L = w_1 I + w_2 K + w_3 A + w_4 S + w_5 D ]

**UI:**

- Species selector（Poultry / Swine / Cattle）
- Infection curves Before/After
- Antibiotic use bar chart
- 24‑hour manure decomposition status

---

### 1.5 Post‑Harvest & Food Loss

**Indicators:**

- Anti‑Spoilage Effect [ \frac{S_{\text{before}} - S_{\text{after}}}{S_{\text{before}}} ]
    
- Food Loss Reduction (t/year) [ Y_{\text{marketable, after}} - Y_{\text{marketable, before}} ]
    
- Shelf‑Life Extension (days)
    

**UI:**

- Product selector（Watermelon / Fruits / Vegetables）
- Spoilage Before/After bar
- Food loss reduction cumulative graph

---

### 1.6 Soil & Climate

**Indicators:**

- ΔC (Soil Carbon Sequestration) [ \Delta C = C_{\text{after}} - C_{\text{before}} ]
    
- CH₄ Reduction (tCO₂e)
    
- N₂O Reduction (tCO₂e)
    
- GHG Reduction: [ (\Delta CH_4 \times GWP_{CH_4}) + (\Delta N_2O \times GWP_{N_2O}) ]
    

**UI:**

- Soil carbon time‑series
- GHG reduction stacked chart
- Map of climate impact intensity

---

### 1.7 PBPE Value & ROI

**Indicators:**

- PBPE‑Biosecurity Value (USD) [ V = L_{\text{reduction}}
    
    - C_{\text{reduction}}
    - Y_{\text{gain}}
    - Q_{\text{premium}}
    - F_{\text{loss reduction}}
    - \text{Climate Credits} ]
- Biosecurity ROI [ ROI = \frac{V - \text{Cost of MBT55}}{\text{Cost of MBT55}} ]
    
- Price Stability Index [ 1 - \frac{\sigma_{\text{after}}}{\sigma_{\text{before}}} ]
    

**UI:**

- Value breakdown donut chart（Loss / Cost / Yield / Quality / Climate）
- ROI gauge
- Price volatility Before/After line chart

### 3.2 Draft

#### 1. Introduction

PBPE‑Marketplace (Planetary Bio‑Positive Economy Marketplace) is a global platform that transforms biological improvements—such as disease suppression, food loss reduction, and climate mitigation—into measurable economic value.

#### 2. Problem Statement

The current food and climate systems suffer from:

- High disease and spoilage losses
- Volatile food prices
- Rising production costs
- Climate emissions from agriculture
- Fragmented financial instruments

Most “green” models rely on **consumer premiums**, which are not scalable in low‑income contexts.

#### 3. PBPE Concept

PBPE is a **bio‑positive economic model** where value originates from:

- Risk reduction
- Cost reduction
- Yield increase
- Quality enhancement
- Food loss reduction
- Climate co‑benefits

No added consumer cost is required.

#### 4. Architecture

- **Modules (Economic Layer):**  
    pbpe‑finance, pbpe‑biosecurity, pbpe‑dashboard, pbpe‑coffee, pbpe‑health
    
- **Engines (Scientific Layer):**  
    mbt‑biosecurity‑engine, agrix‑engine, healthbook‑engine
    
- **Marketplace (Platform Layer):**  
    API, dashboard, reporting, financial products.
    

#### 5. MBT55 & Biosecurity Integration

MBT55 provides:

- Disease suppression
- Spoilage reduction
- Livestock biosecurity
- Soil regeneration
- Climate mitigation

PBPE‑Biosecurity converts these into:

- Biosecurity Credits
- PBPE Value (USD)
- Price Stability Index

#### 6. PBPE‑Biosecurity Credits

A new asset class based on:

- Disease loss reduction
- Spoilage reduction
- Yield gain
- Quality premium
- Cost reduction
- Climate co‑benefits

#### 7. Marketplace & Financial Flows

- PBPE‑Biosecurity Credits are issued based on verified data.
- PBPE‑Finance packages them into financial products.
- PBPE‑Dashboard provides real‑time transparency.
- Investors and foundations fund scalable deployment of MBT55 and related engines.

#### 8. Governance & Partners

- Foundations (Gates, Rockefeller)
- Development banks (World Bank, IFC)
- UN agencies (FAO, IFAD, UNDP)
- Industry partners (coffee, cacao, citrus, tea, livestock)

#### 9. Roadmap

- Phase 1: Coffee & Watermelon pilots
- Phase 2: Multi‑crop, multi‑region deployment
- Phase 3: Full PBPE‑Marketplace with cross‑sector integration
