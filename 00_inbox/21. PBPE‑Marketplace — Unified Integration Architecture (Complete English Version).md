# 🟩 **② PBPE‑Biosecurity Credits (Complete English Version)**

**A New Asset Class Derived from Biological Risk Reduction**

---

## **1. Definition**

PBPE‑Biosecurity Credits are **quantified units of biological value** generated by MBT55‑driven improvements:

- Disease loss reduction
- Spoilage reduction
- Livestock culling avoidance
- Yield increase
- Quality enhancement
- Food loss reduction
- Climate co‑benefits (ΔC, CH₄, N₂O)

These credits represent **real, measurable economic value**, not hypothetical offsets.

---

## **2. Why They Are Different from Carbon Credits**

|Carbon Credits|PBPE‑Biosecurity Credits|
|---|---|
|Based on avoided emissions|Based on avoided losses|
|Often abstract|Directly measurable|
|Single‑dimension|Multi‑dimension (yield, quality, loss, climate)|
|Slow verification|Real‑time verification via PBPE Dashboard|

---

## **3. High‑Level Formula**

```
PBPE-Biosecurity Credit =
   (Disease Loss Reduction Value)
 + (Spoilage Reduction Value)
 + (Yield Gain Value)
 + (Quality Premium Value)
 + (Cost Reduction Value)
 + (Climate Co-Benefit Value)
```
