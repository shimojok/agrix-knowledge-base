# **2. Mechanistic Summary (English)**

### **MBT55 suppresses crop diseases through four core mechanisms:**

1. **Priority Effect (Niche Pre‑Occupation)**  
    MBT55 colonizes leaf and root surfaces first, blocking pathogen attachment.
    
2. **Antagonistic Metabolites**
    
    - Lactic acid
    - Organic acids
    - Short‑chain fatty acids (SCFA)
    - Anti‑fungal peptides
    - Actinomycete‑derived antibiotics
3. **Nutrient & Space Exclusion**  
    MBT55 rapidly decomposes organic substrates, leaving no resources for pathogens.
    
4. **Induced Systemic Resistance (ISR)**  
    MBT55 metabolites activate plant immune pathways.
