```
# PBPE Value Creation Model

The PBPE Value Creation Model explains how biological improvements  
generated by MBT55 are converted into measurable economic value  
through PBPE credits.  
It defines the economic logic that underpins the PBPE Marketplace.

---

# 1. Overview

PBPE creates value by converting:

- Soil carbon gains  
- Methane reduction  
- Nitrous oxide reduction  
- Spoilage reduction  
- Water quality improvement  
- Disease suppression  
- Quality enhancement  

into **standardized, tradable credits**.

---

# 2. Sources of Economic Value

PBPE value is derived from:

## 2.1 Climate Value
- Reduced GHG emissions  
- Increased carbon sequestration  
- Alignment with national climate targets  

## 2.2 Productivity Value
- Higher yields  
- Lower disease losses  
- Improved feed efficiency  
- Reduced spoilage  

## 2.3 Cost Reduction Value
- Lower fertilizer use  
- Lower veterinary costs  
- Lower cold chain losses  
- Lower water treatment costs  

## 2.4 Market Premium Value
- Higher product quality  
- ESG-driven procurement  
- Sustainable branding  

---

# 3. PBPE Credit Value Formula

Each PBPE credit follows:

PBPE_Value = (Climate_Value + Productivity_Value + Cost_Savings + Quality_Premium) × Verification_Factor × Market_Demand_Index


Where:

- **Verification_Factor** adjusts for data quality  
- **Market_Demand_Index** reflects ESG buyer demand  

---

# 4. Multi-Sector Value Integration

PBPE integrates value across:

- Agriculture  
- Livestock  
- Food systems  
- Water systems  
- Supply chains  
- Government climate programs  

This creates a unified economic ecosystem.

---

# 5. Value Distribution

PBPE ensures fair value distribution:

- Farmers receive majority of credit revenue  
- Verifiers receive service fees  
- Enterprises receive Scope 3 reductions  
- Governments receive climate progress  
- Marketplace receives transaction fees  

---

- Methane reduced by 18%

# 7. One-Sentence Summary

**PBPE creates economic value by converting biological improvements  
into standardized, verifiable, and tradable impact credits.**
```

```
# PBPE Pricing and Market Valuation Model

The PBPE Pricing and Market Valuation Model defines how PBPE credits  
are priced, how market value is determined, and how price stability  
is maintained across the PBPE Marketplace.

---

# 1. Overview

PBPE pricing is based on:

- Scientific impact value  
- Market demand  
- Verification level  
- Regional factors  
- Credit scarcity  
- Policy incentives  

PBPE uses a hybrid pricing model combining intrinsic value and market dynamics.

---

# 2. Intrinsic Value Calculation

Intrinsic value is determined by:

Intrinsic_Value = Impact_Value × Social_Cost_of_Carbon × Verification_Weight × Regional_Adjustment


Where:

- **Impact_Value** = tCO₂e or equivalent units  
- **Verification_Weight** = 0.85–1.15  
- **Regional_Adjustment** accounts for local climate policy  

---

# 3. Market-Based Pricing

Market pricing is influenced by:

- Supply and demand  
- ESG buyer interest  
- Government incentives  
- Seasonal biological cycles  
- Credit type scarcity  
- Marketplace liquidity  

---

# 4. Price Stability Mechanisms

PBPE uses:

- Price floors  
- Dynamic ceilings  
- Circuit breakers  
- Market maker incentives  
- Auction-based resets  

These prevent manipulation and excessive volatility.

---

# 5. Credit Rating System (A–D)

PBPE assigns ratings based on:

- Data quality  
- Verification level  
- Methodology robustness  
- Regional risk  
- Historical performance  

Ratings influence price multipliers:

A = ×1.20 B = ×1.00 C = ×0.85 D = ×0.60


---

# 6. Marketplace Valuation Model

Total PBPE market value:

Market_Value = Σ (Credit_Price × Credit_Volume)

Segmented by:

- Soil carbon  
- Methane  
- Nitrous oxide  
- Spoilage  
- Water quality  
- Disease reduction  

---

# 7. Example Scenario

A batch of MRC credits:

- Impact: 412 tCO₂e  
- Intrinsic value: $38/tCO₂e  
- Rating: A (×1.20)  
- Market demand index: 1.14  

Final price:

Price = 38 × 1.20 × 1.14 = $52.0 per credit

---

# 8. One-Sentence Summary

**PBPE pricing combines intrinsic scientific value with market dynamics  
to create stable, transparent, and fair valuation for all PBPE credits.**
```
