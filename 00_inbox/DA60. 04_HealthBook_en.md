# Malaria 3-Layer Defense Model

## Overview

MBT55 provides a **chemical-independent, three-layer defense mechanism** against Plasmodium, the malaria parasite.

---

## The 3-Layer Defense Model

### Layer 1: Invasion Inhibition (Receptor Masking)

- **Specific sugar chains** cleaved from animal-derived medicines by Tateyama-Menami (Atopostipes sp.) mask red blood cell surface receptors
- Physically blocks parasite adhesion and invasion (decoy effect)

### Layer 2: Proliferation Inhibition (Iron Chelation)

- **Siderophores** (iron-binding molecules) produced by Bacillus spp. (Tateyama-ken etc.) compete with parasites for iron in blood and cells
- Starves the parasite's reproductive cycle

### Layer 3: Cytokine Storm Avoidance (Immune Regulation)

- Novel steroid metabolites derived from deer antler suppress excessive immune responses (TNF-α, IL-6)
- Controls the "host hyperinflammatory response" — the primary cause of severe malaria mortality

---

## Complementarity with Gates Foundation's Existing Strategy

| Gates Foundation Approach | MBT55 Complement |
|:---|:---|
| Vaccines (mRNA, etc.) | Gut environment optimization → Resolution of vaccine low-responder problem |
| Insecticide-treated bed nets | Environmental biocontrol of mosquito breeding |
