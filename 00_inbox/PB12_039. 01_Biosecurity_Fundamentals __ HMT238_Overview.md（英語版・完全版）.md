# HMT238 Overview

HMT238 is a high‑performance microbial–mineral complex developed through  
decades of field research in Japan. It integrates **238 microbial species**,  
hot‑spring–derived minerals, and diatomaceous earth to create a  
self‑reinforcing ecological engine for soil, plant, livestock, and water stability.

HMT238 is one of the scientific foundations that informed the development of MBT55.

---

## 1. What HMT238 Is

HMT238 is a **multi‑species, multi‑function microbial consortium** containing:

- 238 microbial species  
- Aerobic and anaerobic bacteria  
- Yeasts and filamentous fungi  
- Actinomycetes  
- Mineral‑reducing microbes  
- SCFA‑producing microbes  
- Humification‑driving microbes  

It is combined with:

- **Diatomaceous earth** (porous silica matrix)  
- **Hot‑spring minerals** (72+ trace elements)  
- **Natural redox catalysts**  

This creates a **microbial–mineral hybrid engine**.

HMT238 was developed through:

- Low‑oxygen environments
