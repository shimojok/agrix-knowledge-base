## 1. Background: The Global Olive Crisis

Olive production—central to Mediterranean culture, economy, and cuisine—faces  
unprecedented biological and climate threats.

### **Major Threats**
- **Xylella fastidiosa** (“olive quick decline syndrome”)  
- **Anthracnose (Colletotrichum spp.)**  
- Soil degradation  
- Drought and heat stress  
- Declining oil content and fruit quality  

Xylella alone has caused:

- **Tens of millions of olive trees to die**  
- Entire regions of Italy, Spain, and Greece to lose productivity  
- Billions of euros in economic damage  

There is **no chemical cure** for Xylella.  
A biological, regenerative solution is required.
