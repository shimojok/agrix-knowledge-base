# 📧 **Formal Invitation Letter to Lloyd Le Page  
— Participation in SIIW International Foundation**

**Subject:** Invitation to Join the SIIW International Foundation as Founding Board Member and Senior Advisor

Dear Lloyd,

I hope you are doing well.  
I am writing to extend a formal invitation for you to join the **SIIW International Foundation (Social Innovation Initiative Worldwide)** as a **Founding Board Member and Senior Advisor for Global Agricultural Systems**.

As you know, my work over the past years has focused on designing **Planetary Social Innovation Systems**—integrated operating systems that connect **agriculture, climate resilience, ecology, water, public health, and finance** into a unified national‑scale architecture.  
This includes:

- **PBPE OS** (Planetary Social Innovation Operating System)  
- **AGRIX** (National Agricultural Digital Twin)  
- **AquaGrid** (Water × Energy × Sanitation Infrastructure OS)  
- **HealthBook** (Preventive Health OS)  
- **MBT55 Hypercycle** (Microbial Ecosystem Engine)  
- **PBPE‑Marketplace** (Multi‑value Climate Finance System)

These systems are now reaching the stage where **international governance, credibility, and implementation leadership** are essential.

---

## **Why Your Leadership Is Essential**

Your experience leading **CGIAR**, **AGRA**, and major agricultural transformation programs across Africa places you among the very few global leaders who understand:

- the realities of national agricultural systems  
- the complexity of climate adaptation  
- the importance of integrating science, policy, and field implementation  
- the operational challenges of scaling innovations across countries  
- the expectations and working style of the Gates Foundation and other global actors  

Your guidance would be invaluable in ensuring that SIIW becomes a **globally trusted institution** capable of supporting governments, international organizations, and private‑sector partners.

---

## **Your Proposed Roles**

### **1. Founding Board Member, SIIW International Foundation**  
Providing strategic oversight, international credibility, and governance leadership.

### **2. Senior Advisor for Global Agricultural Systems**  
Advising on national‑scale implementation of AGRIX, PBPE OS, and AquaGrid,  
and guiding partnerships with African and South Asian governments.

These roles are designed to align with your expertise while allowing flexibility in your level of engagement.

---

## **The Purpose of SIIW**

SIIW aims to become the world’s leading institution for:

- designing **national social operating systems**  
- integrating agriculture, climate, health, water, and finance  
- reducing national social costs  
- supporting governments with evidence‑based transformation models  
- establishing PBPE Credits as an international standard  

Your participation would significantly strengthen SIIW’s legitimacy and impact.

---

## **Closing**

I would be honored to discuss this opportunity with you and explore how we can shape SIIW together as a global platform for agricultural and climate innovation.

Thank you very much for considering this invitation.

Warm regards,  
**Kaz Shimojo**  
Chief Architect, Planetary Social Innovation Systems  
Founder, SIIW International Foundation
