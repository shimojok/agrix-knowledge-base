```
# Agriculture and Soil Regeneration Use Case

This use case demonstrates how MBT55 and PBPE credits transform  
agricultural soil regeneration into measurable financial value.  
Farmers gain new revenue streams while improving soil health,  
carbon sequestration, and climate resilience.

---

# 1. Overview

MBT55 improves:

- Soil carbon sequestration  
- Soil structure and aggregation  
- Water retention  
- Nutrient cycling  
- Microbial diversity  
- Crop resilience  

These improvements generate PBPE credits.

---

# 2. PBPE Credits Generated

Agriculture produces:

- **ΔC‑SCC**（Soil Carbon Credit）  
- **NRC**（Nitrous Oxide Reduction Credit）  
- **DRC**（Disease Reduction Credit）  
- **WQC**（Water Quality Credit）  
- **QPC**（Quality Premium Credit）  

---

# 3. Data Inputs

Required data:

- Soil carbon baseline  
- Fertilizer usage  
- Soil moisture  
- Microbial activity  
- Crop yield  
- Satellite NDVI  
- Lab carbon analysis  

---

# 4. Workflow

1. **MBT55 applied to soil**  
2. **Soil carbon increases**  
3. **GHG emissions decrease**  
4. **Impact data collected**  
5. **PBPE credits issued**  
6. **Credits sold on marketplace**  
7. **Farmer receives revenue**  

---

# 5. Financial Impact

Farmers gain:

- Carbon revenue  
- Fertilizer savings  
- Yield stability  
- Soil resilience  
- Reduced disease losses  

---

# 6. Example Scenario

A 50-hectare farm in Chiba:

- Soil carbon increases by 0.8 tC/ha  
- N₂O emissions drop by 12%  
- Disease incidence drops by 18%  

Credits issued:

- 146 tCO₂e ΔC‑SCC  
- 22 tCO₂e NRC  
- 18 DRC units  

---

# 7. One-Sentence Summary

**PBPE converts soil regeneration into financial value, enabling farmers  
to earn revenue while restoring agricultural ecosystems.**
```

```
# Livestock and Methane Reduction Use Case

This use case demonstrates how MBT55 reduces methane emissions in  
livestock systems and converts these reductions into PBPE credits.  
Farmers gain new revenue streams while improving animal health and  
reducing climate impact.

---

# 1. Overview

MBT55 improves:

- Gut microbiome stability  
- SCFA production  
- Feed efficiency  
- Immune activation  
- Methane suppression  

These improvements generate PBPE credits.

---

# 2. PBPE Credits Generated

Livestock systems produce:

- **MRC**（Methane Reduction Credit）  
- **LHC**（Livestock Health Credit）  
- **FEC**（Feed Efficiency Credit）  
- **AMRC**（AMR Reduction Credit）  

---

# 3. Data Inputs

Required data:

- Baseline methane emissions  
- Feed intake  
- Weight gain  
- Health metrics  
- Manure emissions  
- Respiration chamber data  
- Sensor logs  

---

# 4. Workflow

1. **MBT55 added to feed or water**  
2. **Methane emissions decrease**  
3. **Feed efficiency improves**  
4. **Health metrics increase**  
5. **Impact data collected**  
6. **PBPE credits issued**  
7. **Credits sold on marketplace**  

---

# 5. Financial Impact

Farmers gain:

- Methane credit revenue  
- Feed cost savings  
- Reduced veterinary costs  
- Improved weight gain  
- Lower mortality  

---

# 6. Example Scenario

A 200-head dairy farm:

- Methane reduced by 18%  
- Feed efficiency improved by 7%  
- Somatic cell count reduced by 22%  

Credits issued:

- 412 tCO₂e MRC  
- 200 LHC units  
- 14 FEC units  

---

# 7. One-Sentence Summary

**PBPE converts livestock methane reduction and health improvements  
into financial credits, enabling climate-positive animal agriculture.**
```
