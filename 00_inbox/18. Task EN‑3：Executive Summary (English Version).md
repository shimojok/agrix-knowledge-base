**MBT‑Biosecurity‑Engine — Executive Summary for Global Foundations & Development Agencies**

---

## **1. Global Challenge**

The global food system faces a multi‑layered crisis:

- Crop diseases causing severe yield loss
- Post‑harvest spoilage driving global food waste
- Livestock epidemics leading to mass culling
- Soil degradation and microbiome collapse
- Climate emissions from agriculture (CH₄, N₂O)
- Rising production costs and unstable farmer income

These factors collectively drive **food price volatility**, **supply insecurity**, and **economic instability**—the core concerns of the World Bank, FAO, IFAD, and major philanthropic foundations.

---

## **2. MBT55: A Microbial Biosecurity Engine**

MBT55 is a **120‑species microbial consortium** engineered through the M³‑BioSynergy framework.  
It restores ecological functions that modern agriculture has lost:

- Disease suppression
- Spoilage prevention
- Soil regeneration
- Livestock biosecurity
- Climate mitigation
- Quality enhancement
- Yield increase

MBT55 does **not** rely on chemicals or genetic modification.  
It works by rebuilding the **soil–plant–microbe hypercycle**.

PBPE (Planetary Bio‑Positive Economy) converts MBT55’s biosecurity effects into **real economic value**.

- **Risk reduction** (disease, spoilage, culling)

- **Yield increase**

This forms a **self‑reinforcing economic flywheel**:

