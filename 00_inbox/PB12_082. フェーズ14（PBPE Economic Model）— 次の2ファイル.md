```
# PBPE Incentive and Revenue Sharing Model

The PBPE Incentive and Revenue Sharing Model defines how value generated  
by PBPE credits is distributed among farmers, enterprises, verifiers,  
market operators, and national programs.  
It ensures fair compensation and long-term ecosystem participation.

---

# 1. Overview

PBPE incentives are designed to:

- Reward biological improvements  
- Support smallholder farmers  
- Encourage long-term adoption  
- Maintain verifier independence  
- Ensure marketplace sustainability  

---

# 2. Revenue Sources

PBPE revenue comes from:

- Credit sales  
- Enterprise procurement  
- Government climate programs  
- Insurance payouts  
- Premium quality markets  
- Marketplace transaction fees  

---

# 3. Revenue Distribution Model

Farmer / Producer: 60–75% Verifier: 3–7% Marketplace Operator: 5–10% Data Providers (Labs/Sensors): 2–5% Local Government / Program: 5–15%


Percentages vary by region and credit type.

---

# 4. Incentive Types

## 4.1 Adoption Incentives
- Upfront micro-grants  
- Reduced verification fees  
- Free baseline assessments  

## 4.2 Performance Incentives
- Higher payouts for high-quality data  
- Bonuses for multi-year participation  
- Premiums for A-rated credits  

## 4.3 Risk Mitigation Incentives
- Insurance-backed minimum payouts  
- Stabilized price floors  
- Guaranteed buyer-of-last-resort programs  

---

# 5. Multi-Year Incentive Curve

PBPE rewards long-term participation:

Year 1: Base payout Year 2: +10% loyalty bonus Year 3: +15% performance bonus Year 4+: +20% stability bonus

---

# 6. Example Scenario

A 50-hectare farm:

- Issues 180 PBPE credits  
- Market price: $48/credit  
- Revenue: $8,640  
- Farmer share (70%): $6,048  
- Verifier share (5%): $432  
- Marketplace share (10%): $864  

---

# 7. One-Sentence Summary

**PBPE incentives ensure fair, transparent, and long-term value distribution  
that rewards biological improvement and marketplace participation.**
```

```
# PBPE Risk Adjustment and Insurance Model

The PBPE Risk Adjustment and Insurance Model protects farmers, enterprises,  
and investors from biological, climate, and market volatility.  
It ensures stability and confidence in PBPE credit generation.

---

# 1. Overview

PBPE risk management covers:

- Biological risk  
- Climate variability  
- Market price volatility  
- Verification uncertainty  
- Regional instability  

---

# 2. Risk Categories

## 2.1 Biological Risk
- Crop disease outbreaks  
- Livestock health shocks  
- Water contamination events  

## 2.2 Climate Risk
- Drought  
- Flooding  
- Extreme temperature  

## 2.3 Market Risk
- Price crashes  
- Liquidity shortages  
- Buyer withdrawal  

## 2.4 Verification Risk
- Data gaps  
- Methodology uncertainty  
- Evidence inconsistency  

---

# 3. Risk Adjustment Factor (RAF)

Each PBPE credit receives a RAF:

RAF = Base_Risk × Regional_Risk × Data_Quality_Risk × Climate_Risk

Applied as:

Adjusted_Credit_Value = Raw_Value × (1 − RAF)

Typical RAF range: **0.05–0.25**

---

# 4. PBPE Insurance Mechanisms

PBPE offers:

## 4.1 Minimum Revenue Guarantee (MRG)
- Ensures minimum payout per credit  
- Backed by insurance pool  

## 4.2 Biological Shock Insurance
- Covers disease outbreaks  
- Covers livestock mortality events  

## 4.3 Climate Variability Insurance
- Covers drought/flood losses  
- Index-based triggers  

## 4.4 Market Stability Insurance
- Protects against price crashes  
- Activates circuit-breaker payouts  

---

# 5. Insurance Pool Funding

Sources:

- Marketplace fees  
- Government climate funds  
- ESG investors  
- Reinsurance partners  

---

# 6. Example Scenario

A farmer expected 200 credits but produced 150 due to drought.

Insurance payout:

Shortfall = 200 − 150 = 50 credits Payout = 50 × Guaranteed_Floor_Price

---

# 7. One-Sentence Summary

**PBPE risk and insurance models stabilize credit generation, protect  
producers, and ensure long-term marketplace confidence.**
```
