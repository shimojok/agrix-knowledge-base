## Structural Problems of Double Counting in Carbon Credits / Emissions Trading

Conventional carbon credit markets and emissions trading schemes suffer from the following structural defects.

### ① Double Claiming (Same reduction claimed by multiple parties)
- The same project’s emission reductions are issued credits by different certification schemes (VCS, Gold Standard, national registries) simultaneously.
- Company A and Company B both report the same supplier’s reduction as “their own Scope 3 reduction.”

### ② Double Issuance
- Multiple registries independently issue credits for the same tonne of CO₂.
- International transfers (ITMO) and domestic reductions count the same amount.

### ③ Cascading Double Counting in Scope 3
- Food loss reduction: retailer and food manufacturer claim the same reduction.
- Fertilizer reduction: farmer and buyer both report the same emission reduction under Scope 3.

### ④ Lack of Interoperability Between Registries
- Each country and each scheme has its own independent ledger → globally unique traceability is impossible.
