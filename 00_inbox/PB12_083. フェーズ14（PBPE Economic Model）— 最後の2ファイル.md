```
# PBPE Financial Flows

The PBPE Financial Flows model describes how money, credits,  
and incentives move through the PBPE Marketplace.  
It defines the economic plumbing that connects farmers, enterprises,  
verifiers, insurers, and national climate programs.

---

# 1. Overview

PBPE financial flows include:

- Credit issuance  
- Credit sales  
- Revenue distribution  
- Insurance payouts  
- Verification fees  
- Marketplace fees  
- Government incentive flows  

These flows ensure transparency and economic stability.

---

# 2. Core Financial Actors

- Farmers / Producers  
- Enterprises (ESG buyers)  
- Verifiers  
- Marketplace operator  
- Insurance pool  
- Government climate programs  
- Data providers (labs, sensors)  

---

# 3. Financial Flow Architecture

[Farmer] → Issues Credits ↓ [Marketplace] → Lists Credits ↓ [Enterprise Buyer] → Purchases Credits ↓ [Revenue Distribution Engine] ↓ [Farmer / Verifier / Marketplace / Insurance Pool]

---

# 4. Credit Sale Flow

1. Credits issued  
2. Credits listed on marketplace  
3. Buyer places order  
4. Smart contract executes settlement  
5. Revenue distributed automatically  
6. Registry updated  

---

# 5. Revenue Distribution Flow

Total_Revenue = Credit_Price × Volume

Distributed as:

Farmer: 60–75% Verifier: 3–7% Marketplace: 5–10% Insurance Pool: 5–10% Government Program: 5–15%

---

# 6. Insurance Flow

Triggered when:

- Biological shocks  
- Climate events  
- Market crashes  
- Verification failures  

Insurance payout:

Payout = Shortfall × Guaranteed_Floor_Price

---

# 7. Government Incentive Flow

Governments may:

- Subsidize verification  
- Provide price floors  
- Purchase credits directly  
- Fund insurance pools  

---

# 8. Example Scenario

A buyer purchases 300 PBPE credits at $50 each:

Total Revenue = $15,000 Farmer (70%): $10,500 Verifier (5%): $750 Marketplace (10%): $1,500 Insurance Pool (10%): $1,500

---

# 9. One-Sentence Summary

**PBPE Financial Flows define how money and credits move through the  
ecosystem, ensuring transparency, fairness, and economic stability.**
```

```
# PBPE Market Simulation Model

The PBPE Market Simulation Model provides a quantitative framework  
for forecasting credit supply, demand, pricing, volatility, and  
market stability under different scenarios.  
It supports policy design, investor analysis, and marketplace planning.

---

# 1. Overview

The simulation model evaluates:

- Credit supply growth  
- Buyer demand curves  
- Price elasticity  
- Volatility patterns  
- Insurance pool solvency  
- Regional adoption rates  
- Climate and biological shocks  

---

# 2. Simulation Inputs

- Historical credit issuance  
- Biological impact data  
- Climate variability indices  
- Enterprise procurement trends  
- Government policy incentives  
- Market liquidity  
- Verification throughput  

---

# 3. Simulation Engine Components

## 3.1 Supply Model
Predicts credit issuance based on:

- MBT55 adoption  
- Biological impact  
- Seasonal cycles  
- Regional factors  

## 3.2 Demand Model
Predicts buyer interest based on:

- ESG budgets  
- Regulatory pressure  
- Price sensitivity  
- Credit ratings  

## 3.3 Price Model
Uses:

- Intrinsic value  
- Market demand index  
- Volatility index  
- Risk adjustment factor  

## 3.4 Volatility Model
Includes:

- GARCH-like volatility  
- Seasonal variance  
- Shock events  

## 3.5 Insurance Solvency Model
Simulates:

- Payout frequency  
- Pool balance  
- Reinsurance triggers  

---

# 4. Scenario Types

- **Baseline scenario**  
- **High adoption scenario**  
- **Climate shock scenario**  
- **Market crash scenario**  
- **Policy incentive scenario**  
- **Verifier bottleneck scenario**  

---

# 5. Example Output Metrics

- Expected credit price  
- Price volatility index  
- Supply-demand gap  
- Insurance pool solvency  
- Regional adoption forecast  
- Enterprise procurement forecast  

---

# 6. Example Scenario

Climate shock scenario:

- Drought reduces soil carbon credits by 22%  
- Methane credits unaffected  
- Demand increases due to ESG pressure  

Result:

Price Increase: +18% Volatility: +32% Insurance Payouts: +12%

---

# 7. One-Sentence Summary

**The PBPE Market Simulation Model forecasts supply, demand, pricing,  
and volatility to support strategic planning and policy design.**
```
