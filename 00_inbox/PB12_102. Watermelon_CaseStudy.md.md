## 1. Background: Watermelon Production Under Severe Root Disease Pressure

Watermelon is highly vulnerable to soilborne pathogens, especially in:

- Greenhouse cultivation  
- Continuous cropping systems  
- High-moisture soils  

### **Primary Diseases**
- **Fusarium Wilt (Fusarium oxysporum f. sp. niveum)**  
- **Pythium Root Rot (Pythium aphanidermatum / ultimum)**  
- **Rhizoctonia damping-off**  

These pathogens cause:

- Severe root necrosis  
- Vascular blockage  
- Plant collapse  
- Fruit size reduction  
- High pre-harvest and post-harvest losses  

In China, greenhouse watermelon farms report **up to 40% decay rates**,  
leading to major economic losses.

MBT55/HMT238 has demonstrated the ability to reduce this to **below 5%**,  
representing one of the strongest field results across all crops.

---

MBT55 rapidly colonizes the rhizosphere, displacing:

- *Fusarium oxysporum*

**Result: Pathogens lose ecological space and cannot infect root tissue.**
