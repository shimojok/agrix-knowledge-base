# 📘 **PBPE‑Finance**

### _Financial Layer of the Planetary Bio‑Positive Economy_

---

## 🌍 1. Introduction

**PBPE‑Finance** is the **financial structuring layer** of the  
**Planetary Bio‑Positive Economy (PBPE)**.

Its mission:

> **Convert biological regeneration into investable financial assets.**

PBPE‑Finance transforms:

- Disease suppression
- Food loss reduction
- Soil carbon gains
- GHG reductions
- Livestock biosecurity
- Price stability
- Farmer income uplift

into **Biosecurity‑backed financial products**.

PBPE‑Finance is where **microbial ecology becomes global finance**.

PBPE‑Finance sits between:

- **PBPE‑Dashboard**（economic visualization）

- PBPE‑Biosecurity Value

- Investment products

# 💱 4. PBPE Credit System（世界初の「Biosecurity Credits」）

PBPE‑Finance manages **five classes of PBPE Credits**.

---

## **4.1 Biosecurity Credits（中心資産）**

Generated from MBT‑Biosecurity‑Engine’s 12 KPIs:

- Disease Loss Reduction
- Anti‑Spoilage
- Yield Gain
- Quality Premium
- Livestock Biosecurity
- Food Loss Reduction

These credits represent **risk reduction + productivity gains**.

---

## **4.2 Carbon Credits（ΔC + GHG）**

- Soil carbon sequestration（ΔC）
- CH₄ reduction
- N₂O reduction

These are **MRV‑ready climate assets**.

---

## **4.3 Food Loss Credits**

Generated from:

- Anti‑Spoilage
- Marketable yield increase
- Shelf‑life extension

These credits reduce **Scope 3 emissions** for buyers.

---

## **4.4 Quality Credits**

Generated from:

- Brix
- Polyphenols
- Defect rate

Used by:

- Specialty coffee buyers
- Premium cacao buyers
- Tea & wine industries

---

## **4.5 Price Stability Credits（世界初）**

Generated from:

- Price Stability Index
- Yield CV reduction
- Supply chain resilience

These credits reduce **market volatility risk**.

---

# 🧮 6. Financial Models（models/）

PBPE‑Finance includes **fully functional financial models**.

---

## **6.1 credit_pricing_model.py**

Inputs:

- PBPE‑Biosecurity Value
- ΔC
- GHG reduction
- Food loss reduction

Outputs:

- Credit price（USD）
- Credit volatility
- Credit yield

---

## **6.2 risk_model.py**

Models:

- Supply risk
- Climate risk
- Disease risk
- Price volatility

Outputs:

- Risk‑adjusted credit price
- Portfolio risk score

---

## **6.3 portfolio_model.py**

Tracks:

- AUM
- Diversification
- Sector allocation
- Region allocation
- Risk/return

---

## **6.4 climate_credit_model.py**

Calculates:

- ΔC → tCO₂
- CH₄ → tCO₂e
- N₂O → tCO₂e
- Climate credit valuation

---

## **6.5 price_stability_model.py**

Uses:

- Yield CV
- Price volatility
- Supply resilience

Outputs:

- Price Stability Credit value
