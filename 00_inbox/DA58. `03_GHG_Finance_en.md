# Negative Green Premium

## Bill Gates' "Green Premium" Concept

The Green Premium is the additional cost of choosing an environmentally friendly alternative over a conventional product or service.

---

## MBT55's Achievement of Negative Green Premium

MBT55 is the world's first technology to make the Green Premium **negative**—meaning environmental regeneration is not an "additional cost" but a **profit center.**

---

## Calculation Model

### Base Formula (per 1 kg Nitrogen)

GP = C_MBT - C_conv - ΔE_GHG × P_CO₂ - S_soil × P_CO₂

| Variable | Definition | Example Value |
|:---|:---|:---|
| C_MBT | MBT55 fertilizer cost | $1.50/kgN |
| C_conv | Conventional fertilizer cost | $0.90/kgN |
| ΔE_GHG | GHG reduction | 7.58 kgCO₂e/kgN |
| P_CO₂ | Carbon price | $50/tCO₂e |
| S_soil | Soil carbon storage | 1.00 kgCO₂e/kgN |

---

## Calculation Result

GP = $1.50 - $0.90 - $0.379 - $0.05 = +$0.171/kgN


When carbon price exceeds **$100/tCO₂e**, GP becomes negative (= MBT55 is cheaper).

---

## Negative Green Premium per Hectare

| Item | Conventional | MBT55 |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Chemical Fertilizer Cost | $120/ha | **$19/ha** |
| Carbon Credit Revenue | $0 | **$45/ha** |
| Net Cost | $120/ha | **-$146/ha (Profit)** |

**Achieves 84% cost reduction and a Negative Green Premium.**
