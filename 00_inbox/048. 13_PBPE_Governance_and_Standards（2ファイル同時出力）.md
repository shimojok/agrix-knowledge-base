```
# PBPE Governance Model

The PBPE Governance Model defines the institutional structure,  
decision-making processes, and accountability mechanisms that ensure  
the integrity, transparency, and long-term stability of the PBPE Marketplace.

---

# 1. Governance Principles

PBPE governance is based on:

- Transparency  
- Scientific integrity  
- Multi-stakeholder participation  
- Decentralized verification  
- Fairness and inclusivity  
- Anti-manipulation safeguards  

---

# 2. Governance Bodies

PBPE governance consists of five core bodies:

## 2.1 Steering Committee
Responsible for:

- Strategic direction  
- Policy approval  
- Oversight of marketplace integrity  

Members include scientists, policymakers, and industry leaders.

## 2.2 Technical Advisory Board
Responsible for:

- Methodology updates  
- Scientific validation  
- Data quality standards  

Includes experts in soil science, microbiology, climate science, and AI.

## 2.3 Verifier Council
Responsible for:

- Accreditation of verifiers  
- Verification rule enforcement  
- Dispute mediation  

## 2.4 Farmer & Producer Assembly
Ensures representation of:

- Smallholder farmers  
- Large-scale producers  
- Livestock operators  
- Aquaculture producers  

## 2.5 Enterprise & ESG Stakeholder Panel
Represents:

- Food companies  
- Retailers  
- Financial institutions  
- ESG buyers  

---

# 3. Governance Processes

PBPE uses structured processes for:

- Methodology updates  
- Verification rule changes  
- Marketplace parameter adjustments  
- Smart contract upgrades  
- Dispute resolution  
- Emergency actions  

All changes require multi-party approval.

---

# 4. Voting System

PBPE uses:

- Weighted voting by stakeholder group  
- Multi-signature approval for major changes  
- Transparent vote logs  
- Public governance reports  

---

# 5. Transparency Mechanisms

PBPE ensures transparency through:

- Public registry access  
- Open methodology documentation  
- Verifier accreditation lists  
- Governance meeting minutes  
- Smart contract audit reports  

---

# 6. One-Sentence Summary

**The PBPE Governance Model ensures transparent, science-based,  
multi-stakeholder oversight of the PBPE Marketplace.**
```

```
# PBPE Methodology Standards

The PBPE Methodology Standards define the scientific, measurement,  
and verification rules used to calculate PBPE credits across all  
biological and climate domains.  
They ensure accuracy, comparability, and global acceptance.

---

# 1. Methodology Principles

PBPE methodologies follow:

- Scientific rigor  
- Transparency  
- Reproducibility  
- Conservativeness  
- Data integrity  
- International alignment  

---

# 2. Core Methodology Components

Each PBPE methodology includes:

- System boundaries  
- Baseline definition  
- Measurement protocols  
- Calculation formulas  
- Data quality requirements  
- Verification procedures  
- Uncertainty assessment  

---

# 3. Methodology Categories

PBPE includes methodologies for:

## 3.1 Soil Carbon (ΔC‑SCC)
- Soil sampling  
- Carbon fraction analysis  
- Bulk density measurement  
- Satellite-supported modeling  

## 3.2 Methane Reduction (MRC)
- Respiration chamber data  
- Feed efficiency metrics  
- Enteric methane modeling  

## 3.3 Nitrous Oxide Reduction (NRC)
- Fertilizer application data  
- Soil N₂O flux measurements  
- Emission factor adjustments  

## 3.4 Spoilage Reduction (SRC)
- Shelf-life testing  
- Microbial load analysis  
- Cold chain monitoring  

## 3.5 Water Quality (WQC)
- Ammonia/nitrate levels  
- Pathogen load  
- Organic matter reduction  

## 3.6 Disease Reduction (DRC)
- Pathogen suppression metrics  
- Mortality reduction  
- Immune response indicators  

---

# 4. Data Quality Requirements

PBPE requires:

- Minimum sampling frequency  
- Calibration of sensors  
- Lab accreditation  
- Satellite data validation  
- Chain-of-custody documentation  

---

# 5. Verification Standards

Verification includes:

- Independent third-party review  
- Evidence-based validation  
- Smart contract-backed audit trails  
- Multi-signature approval  
- Randomized spot checks  

---

# 6. International Alignment

PBPE aligns with:

- IPCC guidelines  
- ISO 14064  
- FAO LEAP frameworks  
- GHG Protocol  
- National carbon registry rules  

---

# 7. One-Sentence Summary

**PBPE Methodology Standards provide scientifically rigorous,  
transparent, and internationally aligned rules for calculating  
and verifying PBPE credits.**
```
