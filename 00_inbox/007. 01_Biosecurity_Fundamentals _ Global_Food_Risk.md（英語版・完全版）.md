# Global Food Risk

Global food systems are entering an era of unprecedented instability.  
Climate change, pathogen expansion, soil degradation, and supply‑chain shocks  
are converging to create systemic risk across agriculture, livestock, and food distribution.

Biosecurity is now a global necessity, not an option.

---

## 1. Climate‑Driven Disease Expansion

Rising temperatures and humidity accelerate:

- Fungal diseases (rust, mildew, blight)
- Bacterial outbreaks (Xylella, HLB)
- Viral spread (mosaic viruses)
- Vector expansion (whiteflies, psyllids)

Diseases are appearing in regions where they never existed before.

---

## 2. Soil Degradation and Nutrient Collapse

Global soils are losing:

- Organic matter  
- Microbial diversity  
- Carbon stocks  
- Water retention capacity  

This leads to:

- Yield instability  
- Fertilizer dependency  
- Increased disease susceptibility  

---

## 3. Post‑Harvest Loss and Spoilage

Up to **40% of global food** is lost after harvest due to:

- Microbial spoilage  
- Oxidation  
- Temperature stress  
- Poor storage conditions  

MBT55 reduces spoilage dramatically  
(e.g., watermelon **40% → <5%**).

---

## 4. Livestock Disease and One Health Risk

Livestock systems face:

- Avian influenza  
- African swine fever  
- Foot‑and‑mouth disease  
- Gut dysbiosis  
- Antibiotic resistance (AMR)

These risks threaten food supply and human health simultaneously.

---

## 5. Supply‑Chain Volatility

Global supply chains are vulnerable to:

- Climate shocks  
- Geopolitical disruptions  
- Transportation failures  
- Market speculation  

Biosecurity stabilizes production → stabilizes markets.

---

## 6. Economic Risk

Food instability creates:

- Price volatility  
- Farmer income collapse  
- Export losses  
- Insurance risk  
- National food security threats  

Biosecurity is the **cheapest and most scalable risk‑reduction tool**.

---

## 7. Why MBT55 Matters

MBT55 directly addresses global food risk by:

- Reducing disease pressure  
- Stabilizing yield  
- Regenerating soil  
- Reducing spoilage  
- Improving livestock health  
- Lowering AMR  
- Reducing CH₄ and N₂O emissions  

MBT55 = **Biosecurity × Climate Stability × Economic Resilience**

---

## 8. One‑Sentence Summary

**Global food risk is rising rapidly, and biosecurity—powered by MBT55—provides the most scalable solution for stabilizing food, climate, and economic systems.**
