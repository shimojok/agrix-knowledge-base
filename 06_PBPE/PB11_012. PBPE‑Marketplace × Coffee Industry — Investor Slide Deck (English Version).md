# **Slide 2 — The Problem: Coffee Prices Are Not “Fair Market Prices”**

Today’s coffee prices are driven not by supply–demand fundamentals but by:

- Rising fertilizer, pesticide, and energy costs
- Climate volatility and extreme weather
- Coffee Leaf Rust (CLR) outbreaks
- Declining soil fertility
- Rising logistics and packaging costs
- Quality deterioration and yield instability

**Result:**  
Coffee prices are **cost‑push inflation**, not “true value”.

---

# **Slide 3 — MBT55 / MBT Sustainable Cycle: A Structural Breakthrough**

MBT55 enables:

- **Up to 84% reduction in production cost**
- **2–3× increase in yield**
- **Defect rate reduction: 30–40% → 3%**
- **Quality improvement** (aroma, flavor, color, bitterness)
- **Freshness retention** via increased polyphenols
- **SOC increase → long‑term carbon sequestration**
- **GHG reduction (N₂O / CH₄)**
- **Coffee pulp + regional organic waste → resources**

This is the foundation for PBPE‑Marketplace.
