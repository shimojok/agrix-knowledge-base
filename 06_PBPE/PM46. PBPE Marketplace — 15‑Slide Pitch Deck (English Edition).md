# 📘 **1. PBPE Marketplace — 15‑Slide Pitch Deck (English Edition)**

（そのまま PowerPoint / Keynote / Google Slides に貼れる構成）

---

## **Slide 1 — Title**

# **PBPE Marketplace**

### _The Financial Layer of Planetary Health OS_

Turning real-world climate, agriculture, and health impact into investable value.

---

## **Slide 2 — The Problem**

### _Impact exists everywhere. Value does not._

- Climate actions are not monetized
- Soil regeneration is invisible
- Health improvement has no financial channel
- MRV is slow, manual, expensive
- Carbon markets suffer from double-counting and low trust

**Impact is abundant.  
Value pathways are broken.**

---

## **Slide 3 — The Solution**

# **PBPE Marketplace**

A unified marketplace where **Planetary Bio‑Positive Effect (PBPE)** becomes a new asset class.

- Verified by Planetary Health OS
- Tokenized into PBPE units
- Packaged into financial products
- Invested by institutions
- Settled automatically
- Distributed via 40/40/20

---

## **Slide 4 — How It Works (High-Level Flow)**

```
Real-World Action
   ↓
ImpactIntent (PH‑API)
   ↓
PBPE Value Computed
   ↓
PBPE Financial Products
   ↓
Investors Allocate Capital
   ↓
Settlement & 40/40/20 Distribution
```

---

## **Slide 5 — PBPE: A Universal Unit of Planetary Value**

PBPE quantifies:

- CO₂ reduction
- Soil carbon regeneration
- Water efficiency
- Microbiome improvement (MBT55)
- Medical cost reduction
- Yield stability
- Ecosystem restoration

**One metric across climate, agriculture, and health.**

---

## **Slide 6 — PBPE Financial Product Families**

- **PBPE Carbon Income Bonds**
- **Regenerative Agriculture Bonds**
- **Health Impact Bonds**
- **Soil Carbon Credits**
- **Coffee Resilience Credits**
- **Microbiome Health Credits**
- **Preventive Health Insurance**
- **Agricultural Protection Products**

All standardized under **PBPE‑FIN‑001**.

---

## **Slide 7 — PBPE Impact Engine**

- Integrates Agri / Climate / Health Intents
- Converts Intent outputs → PBPE value
- Forecasts PBPE yield
- Computes volatility
- Feeds PBPE Rating Engine

**The valuation core of the marketplace.**

---

## **Slide 8 — PBPE Registry & Ledger**

Ensures:

- Unique PBPE issuance
- Zero double-counting
- Full traceability
- Settlement records
- Distribution records
- Audit trails

**Solves the trust problem of carbon markets.**

---

## **Slide 9 — PBPE Rating Engine**

Risk model includes:

- Intent Reliability Score (IRS)
- Data Quality Score (DQS)
- Regional Risk Score (RRS)
- PBPE Volatility Index (PVI)
- PBPE Default Probability (PDP)

Outputs: **PBPE‑A / PBPE‑B / PBPE‑C / PBPE‑D**

---

## **Slide 10 — Marketplace API**

Endpoints:

- `/products`
- `/orders`
- `/market/ticker`
- `/settlements/{product_id}`
- `/registry/{pbpe_id}`
- `/portfolio`
- `/portfolio/impact`

**A Stripe‑like API for planetary value.**

---

## **Slide 11 — Settlement & Distribution (40/40/20)**

When PBPE is realized:

- 40% → Actor (farmer, company, individual)
- 40% → PBPE / Climate / Health Funds
- 20% → Platform (BioNexus)

**A regenerative capital loop.**

---

## **Slide 12 — Investor Dashboard**

Investors see:

- PBPE portfolio

- Impact metrics
- ESG alignment
- Settlement history

**Financial + planetary performance in one view.**

---

## **Slide 13 — Issuer Dashboard**

Issuers manage:

- Product issuance
- MRV status
- Settlement events
- Impact reporting
- PBPE yield forecasting

**A complete issuance console.**

## **Slide 14 — Why PBPE Marketplace Wins**

- Unified across climate, agriculture, health
- Automated MRV
- Transparent ledger

- Standardized financial products
- Global interoperability
- Real-time settlement

**A new financial substrate for planetary regeneration.**
