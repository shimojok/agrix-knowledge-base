## **1. Overview**

**PBPE Platform (Planetary Bio‑Phenome Engine)** is the financial and value‑circulation layer of the **Planetary Metabolism Operating System (Planetary OS)**. It transforms **planetary health processes**—soil regeneration, carbon sequestration, food system stability, human metabolic health—into **verifiable, tradable, and investable planetary value units (PBPE).**

PBPE integrates:

- **PBPE Value Engine**
    
- **PBPE Marketplace**
    
- **PBPE Currency Model**
    
- **PBPE Financial Model**
    
- **ANE (AGRIX Nexus EcoSystem)**
    
- **ACAIN (AGRIX Climate‑Agri Investment Nexus)**
    

PBPE is the world’s first system that converts **biological, ecological, agricultural, and health outcomes** into **financial assets, currency units, and sovereign‑grade instruments**.

## **2. Mission**

To establish a **planetary‑scale financial infrastructure** that:

- Measures planetary health
    
- Converts it into standardized PBPE value
    
- Circulates it through global markets
    
- Stabilizes food systems, currencies, and climate finance
    
- Democratizes capital and corrects structural inequality (r > g)
    

PBPE is the **financial engine** of the Planetary Metabolism OS.

## **4. PBPE Value Engine**

The PBPE Value Engine converts **Planetary Health Value** into **PBPE units**.

### **Value Inputs**

- Soil Organic Carbon (SOC)
    
- GHG Reduction (CO₂, CH₄, N₂O)
    
- Food Loss Reduction
    
- Nutrition Improvement
    
- Human Health Improvement (Medical Cost Reduction)
    
- Biosecurity (Disease Suppression)
    
- Food Price Stability
    
- FX Stability Contribution
    

### **Core Formula (Simplified)**

PBPE=f(SOC,GHG,Lf,N,H,Bs,Ps,FXs)

Where:

- SOC: Soil Carbon
    
- GHG: Greenhouse Gas Reduction
    
- Lf: Food Loss Reduction
    
- N: Nutrition Value
    
- H: Health Improvement
    
- Bs: Biosecurity
    
- Ps: Price Stability
    
- FXs: FX Stability Contribution
