# PMOS Constitution の冒頭（正式ドラフト）

## Preamble

> Humanity has achieved unprecedented advances in science, technology, medicine, and agriculture.
> 
> Yet the defining challenges of our time—soil degradation, food insecurity, climate instability, biodiversity loss, chronic disease, and economic inequality—continue to intensify.
> 
> These challenges are commonly addressed as separate sectors.
> 
> We believe they are not separate.
> 
> They are interconnected manifestations of a disrupted Planetary Metabolism.

---

## Core Declaration

> We do not seek to solve agriculture, health, climate, or finance independently.
> 
> We seek to restore Planetary Metabolism,  
> from which food security,  
> biosecurity,  
> human health,  
> climate stability,  
> and economic prosperity naturally emerge.

---

# PMOS Philosophy

PMOSは、

Nature-based Solutionsを否定しません。

One Healthを否定しません。

Circular Economyも否定しません。

Regenerative Agricultureも否定しません。

Climate Financeも否定しません。

しかし、

それらを

**Planetary Metabolism**

という一つの原理で統合します。

---

# PMOS の First Principle

ここを最初の定義として固定します。

## First Principle

> Nature does not recognize the boundaries that human institutions have created.
> 
> Soil,  
> microorganisms,  
> plants,  
> animals,  
> humans,  
> carbon,  
> nutrients,  
> water,  
> and economic activity
