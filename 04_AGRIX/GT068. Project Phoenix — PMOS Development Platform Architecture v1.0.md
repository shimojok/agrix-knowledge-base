# Project Phoenix — PMOS Development Platform Architecture v1.0

## 1. Mission

### Purpose

Project Phoenix is the integrated development platform for building the Planetary Metabolic Operating System (PMOS).

Its purpose is to transform fragmented scientific knowledge, engineering documents, software, and implementation experience into an AI-assisted development environment capable of accelerating innovation in:

- Agriculture
- Human Health
- Climate
- Biosecurity
- Circular Resources
- Planetary Finance

Project Phoenix is not a software project.

It is the development architecture of PMOS itself.

---

# 2. Vision

Project Phoenix creates a continuously evolving knowledge platform where

Human Intelligence

↓

AI Intelligence

↓

Engineering

↓

Deployment

↓

Planetary Impact

become one continuous development cycle.

---

# 3. Design Principles

## Principle 1

GitHub is the Single Source of Truth.

All approved knowledge exists inside repositories.

---

## Principle 2

Obsidian is the Research Workspace.

Ideas mature inside Vault.

Only validated knowledge moves into GitHub.

---

## Principle 3

Pinecone is the Knowledge Index.

It stores searchable vectors.

It never replaces GitHub.

---

## Principle 4

AI assists development.

Humans remain responsible for architecture and decision-making.

---

## Principle 5

Every document becomes reusable knowledge.

No document should exist without contributing to future development.
