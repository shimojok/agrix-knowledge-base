# Lifestyle Risk Engine

The Lifestyle Risk Engine aggregates behavioral and environmental factors
into a unified, normalized risk profile used by HealthBook‑MI.

## Inputs
- Nutrition data (UPF ratio, fiber intake, fermented foods)
- Physical activity (steps, resistance training, sedentary hours)
- Sleep metrics (duration, deep sleep ratio, efficiency)
- Stress indicators (HRV, perceived stress)
- Environmental exposure (PM2.5, chemicals)

## Outputs
- `lifestyle_risk` section of Metabolic Snapshot

## Core Functions
- Normalize raw lifestyle metrics into 0–1 risk scores
- Map lifestyle factors to metabolic pathways (SCFA, inflammation, insulin sensitivity)
- Provide upstream drivers for metabolic inference
