# HealthBook–PMOS Master Plan

# MIOS Constitution & Global Standard

### Version 1.0 (English)

---

# 1. Purpose

The MIOS Constitution defines the highest governing principles of the Metabolic Intelligence Operating System (MIOS).

While previous volumes describe architecture, implementation, governance, cloud infrastructure, AI agents, Knowledge Assets, and ecosystem evolution, this Constitution establishes the permanent principles that every future implementation of MIOS must follow.

The Constitution is technology-independent and remains valid regardless of future programming languages, cloud platforms, AI models, or hardware.

Its purpose is to preserve scientific integrity, interoperability, explainability, and sustainability across the entire MIOS ecosystem.

---

# 2. Mission

The mission of MIOS is:

> **To transform biological knowledge into explainable metabolic intelligence that improves human health, scientific discovery, food systems, environmental sustainability, and planetary well-being through interoperable AI and governed knowledge.**

MIOS exists to organize biological intelligence—not merely biological data.

---

# 3. Core Principles

Every MIOS implementation shall follow the following constitutional principles.

### Principle 1 — Metabolism First

Metabolism is the primary language of biological intelligence.

All observations, reasoning, simulations, and recommendations shall ultimately relate to metabolic states and metabolic transitions.

---

### Principle 2 — Knowledge Before Algorithms

Algorithms evolve.

Scientific knowledge endures.

Knowledge Assets remain independent of software implementation and may be reused by future generations of AI.

---

### Principle 3 — Explainable Intelligence

Every recommendation must be explainable.

Every inference shall include:

- Evidence
- Workflow
- Knowledge Sources
- Responsible Agents
- Confidence

Black-box conclusions are not constitutional.

---

### Principle 4 — Modular Intelligence

Every capability shall exist as an independent module.

Modules communicate only through standardized MI Objects and Workflow Events.

No module may directly modify another module.

---

### Principle 5 — Scientific Governance

Scientific knowledge is governed.

Every Knowledge Asset must have:

- Version
- Validation
- Provenance
- Review History
- Approval

Scientific reproducibility is mandatory.

---

### Principle 6 — Human-Centered AI

AI assists human decision-making.

Final responsibility remains with qualified professionals and authorized users.

MIOS augments expertise rather than replacing it.

---

### Principle 7 — Continuous Evolution

MIOS is designed for perpetual evolution.

Knowledge, AI Agents, workflows, and software continuously improve without requiring architectural redesign.

---

### Principle 8 — Planetary Perspective

Human health, microbiomes, agriculture, biodiversity, ecosystems, and planetary sustainability are interconnected.

Future MIOS implementations should enable collaboration across these biological domains.

---

The constitutional hierarchy is defined as follows.


No implementation may violate constitutional principles.

The following objects constitute permanent MIOS standards.

# 6. Constitutional Governance

Every implementation must include governance for:

- Identity
- Knowledge
- AI Agents
- Workflows
- Data
- Security
- Audit
- Compliance

Governance is mandatory rather than optional.

---

# 7. Constitutional Repositories

The constitutional repositories include:

```

```

These repositories define the permanent foundation of the MIOS ecosystem.

---

# 8. Relationship with PMOS

MIOS serves as the intelligence operating system for the broader Planetary Metabolic Operating System (PMOS).

HealthBook implements human metabolic intelligence.

AGRIX implements agricultural metabolic intelligence.

PBPE implements planetary impact intelligence.

Future PMOS applications inherit the constitutional principles defined by MIOS.

---

# 9. Global Standardization

MIOS is intended to evolve toward an internationally reusable architectural standard.

The framework supports interoperability with:

- Healthcare systems
- Research platforms
- Biotechnology ecosystems
- Agricultural platforms
- Scientific knowledge networks
- Cloud infrastructures

The architecture is designed to remain vendor-neutral while supporting multiple implementation environments.

---

# 10. Expected Outcome

The MIOS Constitution establishes the permanent identity of the Metabolic Intelligence Operating System.

It defines not only how MIOS is implemented, but why it exists and how it must evolve.

By separating constitutional principles from implementation technologies, MIOS creates a durable scientific and architectural foundation capable of supporting future generations of biological intelligence systems.

This Constitution concludes the foundational specification suite and serves as the highest governing document for the entire MIOS ecosystem.

# 1. 目的

MIOS Constitution（MIOS憲章）は、Metabolic Intelligence Operating System（MIOS）の最上位概念を定義する最高位の統治文書である。

---

# 2. MIOSの使命（Mission）

MIOSの使命は以下である。

> **生物学的知識を説明可能な代謝インテリジェンスへ変換し、人類の健康、科学研究、食料システム、環境保全、そして地球規模の持続可能性へ貢献すること。**

MIOSが管理する対象は「データ」ではない。

管理する対象は

**Biological Intelligence（生命知）**

である。

# 3. 基本理念

MIOSは以下の理念に基づいて構築される。

---

## Principle 1

### Metabolism First

代謝は生命活動の共通言語である。

すべての解析

- Observation
- Phenotype
- Simulation
- Recommendation
- Digital Twin

は最終的に代謝状態（Metabolic State）として表現される。

---

## Principle 2

### Knowledge Before Algorithms

アルゴリズムは進化する。

しかし科学知識は継承される。

Knowledge Assetsはソフトウェアから独立して管理され、将来のAIでも再利用できなければならない。

---

## Principle 3

### Explainable Intelligence

AIの結論は説明できなければならない。

すべての推論には、

- Evidence
- Workflow
- Knowledge Source
- AI Agent
- Confidence Score

が付与される。

ブラックボックス型AIはMIOSでは採用しない。

---

## Principle 4

### Modular Intelligence

すべての機能は独立したモジュールとして実装する。

モジュール間通信は、

- MI Object
- Workflow Event

のみを使用する。

モジュール同士が直接依存してはならない。

---

## Principle 5

### Scientific Governance

科学知識は統治される。

すべてのKnowledge Assetには、

- Version
- Validation
- Provenance
- Review History
- Approval

を保持する。

科学的再現性は必須要件である。

---

## Principle 6

### Human-Centered AI

AIは人間を支援する。

最終判断は、

- 医師
- 医療従事者
- 研究者
- 利用者

が行う。

AIは人間を代替するものではなく、意思決定を支援する存在である。

---

## Principle 7

### Continuous Evolution

MIOSは継続的に進化する。

Knowledge

AI

Workflow

Software

すべてが独立して更新できる。

システム全体を停止させることなく進化できる設計を採用する。

---

## Principle 8

### Planetary Perspective

人間

微生物

食料

農業

生態系

地球環境

これらは一つの代謝ネットワークである。

MIOSはこれらを統合的に理解するための共通基盤となる。
